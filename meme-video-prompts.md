## Video 3: "Failed Influencer Moment"
**Duration:** 6 seconds  
**Tone:** Adorable, cringe comedy

**Prompt:**
A scruffy cockapoo is sitting perfectly still, trying to look cool and composed for a selfie video. It has a slight, confident smirk. The dog is clearly attempting to be an influencer - very dramatic, very serious. It does a slow-motion head flip to show off its fluffy ears... but the flip is SO slow and awkward, it gets stuck halfway and just looks confused. The dog's head is sideways at an uncomfortable angle. One eye is looking at the camera, the other is looking away. A single piece of fur sticks straight up. The dog tries to recover by looking cute and winking, but the wink is more of a full-face squint/confused expression. A fly lands on its nose. The dog goes cross-eyed trying to look at the fly. Text overlay: "POV: you're attempting to be aesthetic on social media" or "me trying to take a cute pic for my dating profile"

**Style:** Documentary-style, slow-motion effects, slightly awkward framing that matches the cringe humor, warm color grading.

## How to Use These Prompts

1. **Copy the prompt** to your chosen AI video generator
2. **Add specifications:**
   - Output: 6 second video (360p/720p)
   - Format: MP4
   - Aspect ratio: 9:16 (vertical) or 16:9 (horizontal)
   - FPS: 24fps
3. **Customize:** Add your channel name/watermark request
4. **Generate:** Let the AI create the video
5. **Export & Share:** Download and post to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts

## Tips for Best Results

- Be specific about the dog's appearance (scruffy, cute, cockapoo style)
- Mention animation style (modern AI, smooth, clean)
- Include comedic timing cues
- Specify text overlays you want
- Ask for bright, eye-catching colors
- Request exaggerated facial expressions for maximum humor
